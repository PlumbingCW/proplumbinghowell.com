Howell Township

Howell is the largest municipality in Monmouth County, at about 61 square miles. Neighborhoods such as Ramtown, Oak Glen and the Route 9 corridor are spread across the township, and large parks, including the Manasquan Reservoir and Allaire State Park, take up a good share of its land. Because the township is so spread out, we dispatch from the field and give you an arrival window when you call.

Sewer and water service in Howell varies by street. According to New Jersey American Water, it operates the Adelphia Wastewater Collection System in Howell, which serves about 4,100 accounts. It also provides public water in parts of the township, and other homes use wells and septic systems. We ask which you have, because it changes how we clear a backup or size a water heater.

Lakewood

Lakewood adjoins Howell on the south and is the largest township we serve outside Howell. Our Lakewood emergency plumber page covers ZIP 08701, the local sewer system, the lead service line program and the lake neighborhoods in detail.

Jackson

Jackson is an Ocean County township south of Howell, ZIP 08527. Some properties in the area use private wells and septic systems, and sewer and water service can differ from one street to the next. If your Jackson house has a well, tell us when you call, since a pump, a pressure tank and a filter can cause symptoms that look like a municipal supply problem.

We handle water service lines and well side plumbing as well as the work inside the house.

Freehold

Freehold covers both a borough and a larger surrounding township in Monmouth County, ZIP 07728. CentraState Healthcare System and Freehold Raceway Mall are in Freehold Township, and the Route 9, Route 33 and Route 79 corridors carry most of the traffic, so we plan arrival windows with the road in mind. Retail, medical and office buildings in the area call us for commercial plumbing work, and houses call for water heaters and drains.

Farmingdale

Farmingdale is a small borough that borders Howell, ZIP 07727. Because it is next door, it gets the same dispatch as a Howell address. If the house is older, we look carefully at cast iron drains, steel supply lines and old water heaters. A sewer camera inspection before a purchase is a sensible step on an older property.

Brick

Brick is a large shore side township in Ocean County, ZIPs 08723 and 08724. It lies toward the shore, so we confirm coverage and give you a realistic arrival window when you call. For a scheduled job such as a repiping estimate or a water heater installation, we pick a day that works for both of us.

How we decide where to go

We do not draw a hard line on a map. A call from a street near a border is treated the same as one in the middle of a town, and we send the plumber who is closest and free. If all crews are busy, we tell you so and give you the next window.

When you call from any of these towns, give us the street address, the nearest cross street and whether the house uses public water or a well. If you have a gate code, a locked basement or a pet that needs to be put away, say so. These small details shorten the visit and let the plumber bring the right parts.
