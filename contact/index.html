Call, do not type

A plumbing problem is easier to explain out loud. When you call, we ask what is happening, how fast, and where the water is going. We can often tell you how to slow the damage while the plumber is on the way. The number is (732) 466-1098.

What to have ready when you call

Helpful details

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Whether water is running and where it is coming from.
  • Whether you have closed the main shutoff valve.
  • Whether the house is on public water, a well, sewer or septic.
  • The make and age of the water heater or boiler, if that is the problem.
  • A phone number where the plumber can reach you.

Before the plumber arrives

If water is flowing, close the main valve first. If a drain is backed up, stop using water. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911. These steps limit damage and are covered in our burst pipe repair and sewer line cleaning guides.
