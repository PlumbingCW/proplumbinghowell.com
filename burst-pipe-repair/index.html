What to do in the first five minutes

Stop the damage

  • Close the main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the house, near the meter or the water heater.
  • Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the pipes.
  • Turn off the electric to wet areas if water is near outlets, only if you can reach the breaker without standing in water.
  • Move valuables and rugs out of the wet area.
  • Take photos and video of the damage before you move things.
  • Call the Howell line.

If you cannot find the main valve, the utility can shut off service at the curb in many cases. Learn where your main valve is before you need it, and test that it turns.

Why pipes burst

Freezing is the best known cause. Water expands as it turns to ice and splits the pipe, usually where the ice plug forms and not where it first froze. Pipes in exterior walls, unheated crawlspaces, garages and attics are the usual targets. Corrosion, high pressure and worn fittings also cause bursts at any time of year.

Frozen but not yet burst

If a pipe is frozen and the faucet gives only a trickle, do not use a torch. Open the faucet, warm the area with a hair dryer or a space heater placed safely, and start at the faucet end. Call us if you cannot find the frozen section or if you see any bulge or crack. A pipe that has frozen once may leak when it thaws.

How a burst pipe repair runs

Stop and assess

We confirm the water is off, drain the line and find the failed section.

Repair

We cut out the damaged section and replace it with new pipe and fittings.

Pressure test

We restore water and watch every joint and the meter for movement.

Document

We give you a short written summary and photos for your insurance agent.

After the pipe is repaired, wet drywall, insulation and flooring need to dry quickly to avoid mold. A water damage restoration company handles that step. If the burst pipe was part of a pattern of leaks, ask us about repiping. If you are not sure the leak is fully found, our leak detection service can help.

What affects the cost of burst pipe repair

The cost depends on where the pipe failed and how hard it is to reach.

LocationA pipe in an open basement costs less than one behind a finished wall.
Pipe material and lengthLonger failed runs and costly pipe cost more.
Access and openingsWe cut the smallest hole that works, and you may need drywall repair afterward.
Emergency hoursNights, weekends and holidays cost more than a scheduled repair.
