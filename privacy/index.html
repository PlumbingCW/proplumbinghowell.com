Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Howell Pro Plumbing collects when you visit this site or call our line, and how we use it. Effective November 16, 2026.

Who we are

Howell Pro Plumbing, 4799 US Highway 9 North, Howell, NJ 07731. You can reach us at (732) 466-1098 or contact@proplumbinghowell.com.

Information from phone calls

When you call, we collect your phone number, your name and address if you give them and a description of the problem. We use that information to dispatch a plumber, to follow up on the visit and to improve our service. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We tell you this on the site and you can end the call at any time.

Information from this website

This site has no forms, accounts or comment sections, so it does not collect information you type. Our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited in server logs. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests files from Google servers and Google may receive your IP address. The site embeds Google Maps, which may also set cookies when the map loads.

How we share information

We share your information with the plumber assigned to your job. We may share it with service providers who help us run our phone line and our systems, and with authorities when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep call records and job information for as long as we need them to serve you, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes, and then delete or anonymize them.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, by calling or emailing us. New Jersey residents may have additional rights under state law, and we will respond as the law requires.

Children

This site and our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date when we do.
