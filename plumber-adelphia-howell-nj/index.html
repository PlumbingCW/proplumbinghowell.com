About Adelphia

Adelphia has a shopping center, Adelphia Plaza, on Adelphia-Farmingdale Road, and the Howell Park and Ride. The Adelphia Fire Company serves most of northern Howell. Because the fire district is larger than the locality, we use it only as a reference and do not treat it as the neighborhood boundary.

Mixed use areas generate both residential and light commercial plumbing calls. Homes need drains, water heaters and leaks handled. Shops and offices often need grease control, restroom fixtures and after hours plans, which our commercial plumbing page explains.

The Adelphia sewer system

New Jersey American Water states that it operates the Adelphia Wastewater Collection System in Howell Township. The utility reports about 4,100 accounts and roughly 27 miles of main. That is a real, documented system, and it covers part of the township, not all of it.

Other Howell properties use private septic systems. We could not find a public map that tells a homeowner which side of that line a particular house is on. Check with the utility or the township, and tell us when you call.

Local note: who owns which pipe

In general, a utility owns the main in the street and the property owner owns the pipe from the house to the connection. Boundaries vary, so confirm with New Jersey American Water if you are unsure where your responsibility starts.

Plumbing work in New Jersey follows the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code adopted under N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. When a backup happens, a plumber can clear the house line and view it by camera. If the blockage is in the utility main, the utility is the right contact.

What to do when a drain backs up

First steps for a sewer backup

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, toilets and the washing machine.
  • Keep children and pets away from the affected area.
  • Note whether several fixtures are affected or only one.
  • Locate the cleanout if you know where it is.
  • Call a plumber and explain what you see.

If several fixtures back up at once, the main line is usually involved. Sewer line cleaning restores flow, and a sewer camera inspection shows the cause. Hydro jetting is an option when grease or scale has built up.

Rental and small commercial properties

Landlords and shop owners deal with the same pipes as homeowners, but with tenants, opening hours and sometimes grease to manage. Restaurants and food shops should keep grease out of the drain and have interceptors cleaned on a regular schedule. A line that clogs during business hours costs more than one cleared on a quiet morning.

Keep a written note of your shutoff valve locations and cleanout positions, and share it with staff or tenants. When a problem starts, the first few minutes matter most, and a person who knows where to close the water can limit the damage.
