Plumbing Installation in Howell, NJ

Plumbing installation in Howell, NJ covers new and replacement fixtures, shutoff valves, supply and drain lines, laundry hookups and the plumbing for a bathroom or kitchen remodel. We set each fixture, connect it and test it before we leave.

You can supply the fixture or let us help you choose one. We tell you early if the house needs changes, such as a larger drain or a new shutoff valve, so there are no surprises on the day.

Fixture installation, step by step

Check the rough in

We confirm the drain and supply locations suit the fixture you chose.

Prepare

We shut off water, remove the old fixture and clean the connection.

Set and connect

We set the new fixture, connect supply and drain and seal it where needed.

Test

We run water, check every joint for dryness and leave the work area clean.

Many fixture problems come from the parts you cannot see. An old shutoff valve that will not close, a corroded supply line or a drain that is a half inch off can turn a simple swap into a longer job. We look at those before we start, so we can tell you what is needed.

Bathroom and kitchen remodels

A remodel needs a plumber at two points. The first is rough in, before walls are closed, when drains and supply lines are moved or added. The second is finish, after tile and cabinets are in, when fixtures are set. We coordinate with your contractor on the order of work and the permit. If you are doing the remodel yourself, we can handle only the plumbing parts that need a licensed plumber.

  • Which fixture model you chose, or whether you want help choosing one.

  • Whether the job involves moving a drain or only swapping a fixture.
  • Whether a permit is needed, which we help you find out.
  • Whether a contractor or tile setter needs the plumbing done by a certain date.

Howell note: permits and code

New Jersey uses the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code. Replacing a fixture in the same place is different from adding a fixture or moving a drain, and the township decides which work needs a permit. In Howell, the Construction Office and its plumbing subcode official work from the municipal building on Preventorium Road, and they can tell you what applies.

Our licensed plumbers pull the permit when it is required and we tell you the cost before work starts. If you have a private well or septic system, tell us. Adding fixtures or changing the load can affect those systems, and some jobs need a septic professional to review the plan.
