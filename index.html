What to do in the first five minutes

Stop the damage while you call

  • Close the main water shutoff, usually near the meter or the water heater.
  • Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the pipes.
  • Shut off power to wet areas only if you can reach the breaker while standing dry.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets if a drain is backing up.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911.
  • Take photos before you move anything, for your insurance agent.

We cover each case in detail on our burst pipe repair and sewer line cleaning pages, and the Howell line is open while you read them.

The plumbing problems we see around Howell

Clogged and slow drains are the most common call, followed by water heater failures and hidden leaks. A drain that clogs again within weeks usually needs a camera look and sometimes hydro jetting. A water heater that has run for about a decade is often better replaced than repaired, and our water heater replacement page explains how that decision goes. When a bill climbs with no explanation, leak detection finds the cause before anyone opens a wall.

Older homes bring their own problems. Cast iron drains scale and crack, old steel pipes clog from the inside and copper can pit over time, which leads to the repeated leaks that our repiping team replaces. Kitchens and bathrooms need work too, from faucet repair to toilet repairs, and we handle basements with sump pump service and waterproofing advice.

Howell note: sewer, septic and where to ask

Sewer service in Howell is not the same on every street. New Jersey American Water states that it operates the Adelphia Wastewater Collection System in Howell Township, which serves about 4,100 accounts, and other properties use private septic systems. A 2015 report on the Freewood Acres neighborhood, off Route 9 near I-195, described septic systems and a sewer connection that reached only part of its 620 lots.

Tell us which system you have, since it changes how we clear a backup.

For permits and code questions, the township Construction Office and its plumbing subcode official work from the Howell municipal building on Preventorium Road. New Jersey uses the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code, and your plumber should tell you when a job needs a permit.
