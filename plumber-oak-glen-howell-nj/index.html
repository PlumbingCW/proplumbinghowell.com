Where Oak Glen is

Oak Glen Park sits across Old Tavern Road from the Howell Municipal Complex, which houses the police headquarters at 300 Old Tavern Road. The park has athletic fields and a playground. Oak Glen Road carries the area's name and uses the 07731 ZIP code, which is shared with the rest of Howell.

We could not find an official boundary for Oak Glen. We treat it as a locality around those anchor roads and do not claim exact streets. If you are unsure whether your street counts, just call and say where you live.

Why proximity to the Municipal Complex helps

The township construction office and plumbing permit questions are handled through municipal departments. Jobs such as water heater replacement, a moved fixture or new piping often need a permit, and your plumber should tell you before work begins. New Jersey applies the 2021 National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, so the rules are set at the state level and enforced locally.

Storm damage and plumbing

Tornadoes in June 2017 struck in and around Oak Glen Park and brought down trees. A storm that topples a tree can also break a buried water line or a sewer lateral near its roots. If you see a new wet spot or a sudden drop in water pressure after a storm, close the main valve and call. Our water service line repair page explains how a buried line is located.

Garbage disposals and kitchen drains are another common call in family homes. Never pour grease down a drain, and use cold water when running a disposal. The garbage disposal repair page covers jams and leaks.

Cold weather checks for family homes

Pipes in unheated garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls are the usual freeze risks in a New Jersey winter. Before a hard freeze, disconnect garden hoses, close the shutoff to each outdoor spigot and open cabinet doors on exterior walls so warm air reaches the pipes.

If a pipe freezes, keep the faucet open, warm the area with a hair dryer or heater placed safely nearby and never use an open flame. If a pipe has already split, close the main valve at once and call. Our burst pipe repair page explains how a split is repaired.
